CEO Agents

A multi-agent system designed for strategic decision making. You pass in a brief (prompt), to your CEO agent, which orchestrates a discussion with its board of advisors (custom agents). They debate, converse and come to a consensus. The output is a structured memo with ranked recommendations, dissent, and next actions. With CEO Agents it's not just you or your team making critical decisions. It's you, your team, and a country of geniuses in a data center.

Infinite UI Agents

An infinite brand consistent UI generation system. Define a brand identity once in YAML then deploy 14 specialized agents across 4 teams (Setup, Brand, UI Generation x3, Validation x3) to generate UIs at high scale and quality. The Orchestrator delegates to specialized teams, each with a Lead and domain locked Workers, so Setup validates structure, Brand enforces visual identity, and parallel UI Generation teams produce components concurrently while Validation teams verify compliance. Every agent has one job, and the hierarchy lets you scale generation without losing brand coherence.

The Verifier Agent

A two-agent observer pattern for specialized validation that reduces the review constraint of agentic engineering. A normal Builder runs in your terminal while a sibling Verifier independently re-runs every claim using deterministic domain tools. The Verifier is read-only by architecture, with defense-in-depth layers (persona allowlist, bash-call hook, read-only script mode) making state mutation impossible by design. Specialize one verifier per domain (SQL, Python, UI, deploy, image, or your own); after each builder turn it auto-checks the work and prompts concrete corrections back, looping up to three times before escalating to you. Reclaim the half of your day spent hand-checking "done" and turn every unverifiable gap into a templated check that compounds across sessions.
