# Product Overview — BlendStack

## What We Are
BlendStack is a DTC supplement company. Users build customizable supplement stacks through our app, and we manufacture and ship monthly.

## How It Works
1. User selects from 40+ ingredients (vitamins, minerals, adaptogens, aminos)
2. Our algorithm validates combinations for safety and efficacy
3. We manufacture to order in our Utah facility (FDA-registered, GMP-certified)
4. Ships in branded sachets — 30-day supply, auto-renews monthly

## Core Differentiator
Personalization at scale. Competitors sell fixed formulas. We let users design their own blend, backed by a dose-checking engine that prevents harmful interactions. No other DTC supplement brand does this with custom manufacturing.

## Key IP
- Blend engine: Python microservice with 40+ ingredient interaction rules, dose optimization, and safety validation
- Custom manufacturing process: batch-of-one production line built in-house
- 18 months of user blend data (anonymized) showing which combinations correlate with retention

- Average order value: $45/month

- Annual plan discount: 20% off monthly price
