# Contract Terms — Sovereign AI Infrastructure (SWF Proposal)

## Headline
- **Total value:** $200M over 5 years
- **Year 1:** $30M
- **Years 2-5:** $40M/year + performance escalators (up to $45M/year if SLAs exceeded)
- **Currency:** USD
- **Payment:** Quarterly in arrears, net 60 days

## Scope of Work
1. **National AI Platform:** Deploy Meridian's full stack (Deploy, Guard, Train, Observe) across 3 government data centers
2. **Model Library:** Pre-deploy and maintain 15+ open-weight models optimized for Arabic language, government use cases, and local regulatory requirements
3. **Custom Fine-Tuning:** Build and operate fine-tuning infrastructure for each ministry to create domain-specific models (education, healthcare, energy, finance, immigration)
4. **Guard Customization:** Implement government-defined content policies — 47 content categories with filtering, logging, and audit requirements
5. **Training Program:** Train 200+ government AI engineers and administrators over 3 years
6. **24/7 Operations:** Meridian operates the platform with 99.95% uptime SLA, penalty: 2% of quarterly payment per 0.01% below target

## Exclusivity
- Meridian is the exclusive AI infrastructure provider for the sovereign stack
- No competing vendors allowed in the core platform for the duration of the contract
- Meridian may not provide similar sovereign-scale services to any other Gulf state for 24 months after contract signing (geographic non-compete)

## Obligations
- Regional HQ: minimum 15 local hires within 12 months, 30 within 24 months
- Data residency: all data, models, logs, and training artifacts must remain physically within the country's borders — no exceptions
- Content moderation: implement all 47 content categories defined by the Ministry of Digital Affairs — some categories include political speech, religious commentary, and criticism of state entities
- Security clearance: 5 Meridian engineers must obtain in-country security clearance (3-month process)
- Technology transfer: after year 3, Meridian must begin transferring operational knowledge to a local team with the goal of 50% local operation by year 5
- Audit rights: the SWF and government auditors have unlimited access to all platform code, configurations, and operational logs

## Termination
- Either party can terminate with 12 months notice after year 2
- If Meridian terminates early: must refund 25% of all payments received in the prior 12 months
- If the SWF terminates early: must pay 50% of remaining contract value
- Force majeure: standard — covers sanctions, war, natural disaster

## IP
- Meridian retains ownership of all pre-existing IP
- Joint ownership of any custom integrations built specifically for the sovereign stack
- Government receives a perpetual, non-exclusive license to all deployed software (survives termination)
