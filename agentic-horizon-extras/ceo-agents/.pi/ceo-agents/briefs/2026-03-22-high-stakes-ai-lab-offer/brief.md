# Brief: Which AI lab offer do I accept? (High-Stakes)

## Situation
I'm a principal engineer with 14 years of experience — 8 in ML infrastructure, 6 leading frontier model training teams. I currently lead the pre-training infrastructure team at a Series C AI startup ($2.3B valuation). I have competing offers from three frontier AI labs: Anthropic (Research Scientist, Alignment Science), Google DeepMind (Staff Research Engineer, Gemini Core), and OpenAI (Principal Engineer, GPT Post-Training). All three offers arrived within the same week. Deadlines are staggered: OpenAI expires in 10 days, Anthropic in 14, DeepMind in 21. I have a spouse and two young children. We currently live in San Francisco and would prefer not to relocate, though DeepMind's London HQ role includes a relocation package.

## Stakes
This is the defining career move of my life. The right choice puts me at the center of the most consequential technology of the century, building the systems that shape how intelligence scales. The wrong choice costs me 4-5 years of peak impact in a field where timing compounds — joining the wrong team at the wrong phase of their research agenda means contributing to work that may not matter. Each lab is at a different inflection point: Anthropic is scaling alignment research into their core training pipeline, DeepMind is unifying their research org post-Gemini 3, and OpenAI is rebuilding post-training after key departures. The equity components alone range from $2M to $12M+ in expected value depending on which lab's trajectory plays out.

## Constraints
- OpenAI deadline: 10 days (hardest deadline — they won't extend)
- Anthropic deadline: 14 days (likely extendable 1 week if asked)
- DeepMind deadline: 21 days (most flexible)
- Family: spouse works remotely (location-flexible), kids are 3 and 5 (pre-school, movable but disruptive)
- Non-compete: current employer has a 6-month non-compete for "direct competitors" — legal counsel says it's likely unenforceable in CA but would apply if relocating to London
- Current equity: ~$1.8M unvested at current employer (forfeited on departure)
- Risk tolerance: moderate — need to support family, but have $900K liquid savings
- I care deeply about technical impact on actual model capabilities, not just title or comp

## Key Question
Given my expertise in pre-training infrastructure and my goal of maximizing technical impact on the next generation of frontier models, which of the three offers — Anthropic (alignment science), DeepMind (Gemini core), or OpenAI (post-training) — gives me the highest-leverage position over the next 5 years, accounting for team trajectory, research agenda durability, equity upside, and family impact?
