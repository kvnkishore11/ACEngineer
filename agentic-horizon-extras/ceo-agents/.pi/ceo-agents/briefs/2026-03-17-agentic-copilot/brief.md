# Brief: Should we build an autonomous engineering copilot?

## Situation
We are a mid-stage dev tools company with 500 paying customers and $2M ARR. Our core product is a code review platform. The market is moving fast toward agentic AI tools that can plan, edit, test, and ship code autonomously. Three competitors have announced agentic copilot products in the last 60 days.

## Stakes
If we get this right, we could 3x our market position by owning the agentic layer on top of our existing code review workflow. If we get it wrong, we burn 3-6 months of engineering capacity on a product that doesn't convert, while competitors solidify their positions.

## Constraints
- Engineering team: 8 developers, 2 of whom have agent experience
- Budget: $500K for the next 6 months
- Current product must not regress — we can't go dark on code review
- Enterprise customers require SOC2 compliance for any autonomous actions
- We have no ML/AI team — we'd be building on top of foundation model APIs

## Key Question
Should we invest in building an autonomous engineering copilot, and if so, what's the right first move — full autonomy, scoped automation, or something else entirely?
