# Acquisition Terms — Datadog Offer

## Headline
- **Price:** $350M cash + stock mix
- **Structure:** 70% cash ($245M), 30% Datadog stock ($105M in DDOG shares at 10-day VWAP)
- **Stock lockup:** 12-month lockup on Datadog shares post-close

## Deal Mechanics
- 45-day exclusivity period for due diligence
- Board approval required: 3 of 5 directors
- Customary representations, warranties, and indemnification
- Expected close: 90 days from signing LOI (antitrust review unlikely given market size)
- Working capital adjustment: standard peg to trailing 3-month average

## Founder Terms
- Both co-founders required to sign 2-year employment agreements with Datadog
- Title: VP-level within Datadog's Data Platform division
- Base: $400K/year each
- Retention bonus: $5M each, paid in equal installments over 24 months (forfeited if voluntary departure)
- Non-compete: 2 years post-departure from Datadog (California + New York)

## Employee Terms
- Engineering team (120 people): all offered employment at Datadog, roles preserved for 12 months
- Sales, CS, G&A: "best efforts" to place — no guarantees beyond 90 days
- Unvested employee options: accelerated to vest at close, converted to Datadog RSUs at deal ratio
- Key employee retention pool: $8M allocated by Datadog for VP-level and critical IC retention (3-year vest)

## What Datadog Wants
- The real-time lineage engine integrated into Datadog's APM and infrastructure monitoring stack
- The Fortune 500 enterprise relationships (particularly the 12 customers on 3-year agreements)
- The engineering team — specifically the graph engine and instrumentation SDK teams
- The 3 patents

## What Datadog Does NOT Want
- The Cloudvault brand (will be deprecated within 12 months)
- The sales team (Datadog has its own enterprise sales org)
- The office lease (Datadog will absorb remote employees or relocate to NYC)
