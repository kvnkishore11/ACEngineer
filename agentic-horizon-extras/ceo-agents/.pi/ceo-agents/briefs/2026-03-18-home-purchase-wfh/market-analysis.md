# Market Analysis — Austin TX Housing + Remote Engineering (March 2026)

## Austin Housing Market Snapshot
- Median home price: $425,000 (up 4% YoY)
- Median price in target range ($350-450K): Inventory tightening, 15% fewer listings than Q1 2025
- Average days on market: 28 days (down from 42 in Q3 2025)
- New construction: slowing — permits down 12% YoY
- Rental vacancy rate: 5.8% (healthy, plenty of 2BR options)
- Avg 2BR rent: $2,350-$2,600/month depending on area

## Interest Rate Environment
- Current 30yr fixed: 6.75%
- 6 months ago: 7.1%
- Fed guidance: 2-3 more cuts expected through 2027
- Market consensus for Q1 2027: 5.5-6.0%
- Uncertainty: inflation data could delay cuts
- Historical context: 6.75% is above the 20-year average (4.9%) but below the 40-year average (7.8%)

## Austin for Remote Engineers
- Tech community: strong — Capital Factory, Austin Tech Alliance, multiple meetup groups
- Internet: Google Fiber and AT&T Fiber available in most target neighborhoods (1Gbps symmetric)
- Cost of living: 2% above national average, but 35% below SF and 28% below Seattle
- No state income tax — $195K base saves ~$9,000-13,000/year vs California
- Airport: AUS direct flights to SFO, SEA, NYC (relevant if company does quarterly on-sites)
- Power grid: improved since 2021, but still a consideration — whole-home UPS recommended for WFH

## Remote Work Trends (Engineering)
- 68% of senior engineers work fully remote (2026 survey data)
- Companies offering remote: holding steady at ~60% of tech postings
- Hybrid mandates: increasing at FAANG, but mid-market and startups remain remote-friendly
- Risk factor: if current employer mandates hybrid, nearest office is in SF
- Mitigation: Austin has strong local tech job market — could switch employers without moving

## Neighborhoods in Target Range

### North Austin (Option A territory: $350-400K)
- Areas: Wells Branch, Pflugerville, Round Rock south
- Pros: More space per dollar, newer builds, good fiber coverage
- Cons: Farther from downtown tech scene, less walkable
- Office suitability: Excellent — larger homes, quiet subdivisions

### South Austin (Option B territory: $400-450K)
- Areas: Manchaca, Circle C (edges), Slaughter Lane corridor
- Pros: Closer to downtown, better food/culture access, established neighborhoods
- Cons: Higher prices, some areas lack fiber, older homes may need renovation
- Office suitability: Good — garages often convertible to workshop/lab space

## Opportunity Cost of Waiting
- Austin home prices increasing ~4% annually
- $385K home in March 2026 = ~$400K by Q1 2027
- Additional $15K in price offsets ~$182/month rate savings
- Break-even on waiting: ~82 months (nearly 7 years) to recoup the price increase through lower payments
- Meanwhile: 9 more months of rent ($18,900-$23,400) with zero equity
