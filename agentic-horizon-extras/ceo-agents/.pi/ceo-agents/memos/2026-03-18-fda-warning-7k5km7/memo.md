# Board Memo: FDA Warning Letter Response

**Session:** 7k5km7  
**Date:** March 18, 2026  
**Company:** BlendStack  
**Decision:** How to respond to the FDA warning letter without killing growth  
**Board:** Revenue, Product Strategist, Technical Architect, Contrarian, Compounder, Moonshot  
**Vote:** 6-0 consensus on phased relaunch approach  

---

## Decision Summary

![Decision Framework](./decision-framework.svg)

The board unanimously endorsed a **phased relaunch strategy** that pulls all flagged content immediately, builds genuinely new creative around BlendStack's personalization engine (not word-swapped versions of old ads), targets a 10-14 day relaunch of 1-3 counsel-cleared ads, and constructs the full compliant creative library over weeks 3-8. The IRB clinical study protocol is deferred to Month 2 for design scoping ($5-7K), not Month 1.

Two alternative paths were explicitly rejected:
- **7-day surgical copy swap** — fails FDA net impression doctrine. Same visual frame (stressed→calm) carries the implied disease claim regardless of text changes. Second warning letter risk unacceptable.
- **6-8 week full dark period** — concedes ~$34K in lost LTV over 90 days. Unnecessary when genuinely new creative (different visual logic, not word-swapped) can clear counsel review in 10-14 days.

---

## Final Recommendations (Ranked)

### 1. Phased Relaunch with New Creative Identity (APPROVED — Unanimous)

**Days 1-2:** Pull all 9 flagged assets across Instagram and blog. Engage FDA-specialized regulatory counsel (not generalist). Double referral incentive from $10 to $20. Kill influencer spend ($110 CAC). Reallocate Instagram budget to Google Search.

**Days 3-7:** Write new creative brief built around the personalization engine — blend UI, ingredient transparency, combination checker. Counsel reviews the brief on Day 3 (not Day 12). Freelance compliance auditor ($3-5K) runs full-channel claim inventory across all surfaces: email, in-app, packaging, SMS, influencer briefs.

**Days 8-14:** Counsel reviews full creative unit (visual + copy + CTA together — not just text). 1-3 static ads relaunch as soft launch. File corrective action plan with FDA showing implemented remediation.

**Weeks 3-8:** Full compliant creative library built (15-20 ads). SEO content restarts. Ingredient transparency library v1 begins. IRB protocol design scoped ($5-7K).

**Estimated cost:** $38-50K total over 90 days  
**Estimated lost LTV:** ~$8-11K over 90 days  
**Dark period:** 10-14 days (subset relaunch), full library by week 8

### 2. Compliance Governance Infrastructure (APPROVED — Integrated into #1)

Build permanent compliance architecture to prevent recurrence:
- Two-page claim taxonomy (structure/function vs. disease claims)
- Five-question pre-publish checklist for all content
- Content register logging every live claim, approval date, approver
- Named compliance gate: nothing goes live without clearing the process
- Compliance consultant retainer ($3-5K/month) as permanent pre-publish reviewer

### 3. Clinical Evidence Program — IRB Protocol (APPROVED — Deferred to Month 2)

- **Month 2:** Retain IRB consultant for protocol design ($5-7K). Define endpoints, draft consent language, identify IRB board.
- **Month 3-4:** Protocol submitted. IRB approval. Formal prospective study begins.
- **Exception:** If regulatory counsel advises on Day 1 that an IRB protocol in progress strengthens the corrective action plan, accelerate to immediate.

## Dissent and Unresolved Tensions

### Partially Resolved
1. **Relaunch timeline (7 days vs. 14 days vs. 8 weeks):** Resolved at 10-14 days. Revenue conceded on net impression doctrine. Compounder conceded that new creative (not word-swap) clears the red line. Technical Architect proposed the synthesis.

2. **IRB timing:** Resolved at Month 2 design phase. Moonshot revised from Day 8 to Month 2 after cash concentration argument. Compromise: $5-7K design engagement, not full $15-20K protocol submission.

### Unresolved
1. **Compliant creative conversion rate:** Nobody knows whether personalization-led creative converts at 70% or 40% of disease-claim creative. This is the single largest unpriced variable. The soft launch in Days 10-14 is the measurement mechanism, but if CAC on compliant creative tracks above $95 for 30 days, the Instagram channel may be permanently restructured as a smaller part of the mix. Revenue flagged this explicitly.

2. **Audit scope:** The full-channel audit may surface 30+ violations beyond the 9 cited. The corrective action plan must disclose all findings — Contrarian and Compounder were emphatic. But a longer violation list changes the compliance cost estimate and timeline. This is discovered in Days 3-7, not controllable in advance.

3. **Governance longevity:** Technical Architect designed the compliance architecture. Contrarian warned it becomes shelf documentation within 90 days without a named permanent owner. The $3-5K/month compliance consultant retainer addresses this, but represents an ongoing cost the business hasn't budgeted for.

## Deliberation Summary

The board convened to address an FDA warning letter citing 9 health claims across BlendStack's Instagram ads and blog posts. The core tension was sharp: the flagged Instagram ads drive 38% of new subscriber acquisition, but inadequate response risks FDA escalation to injunction or product seizure — an existential outcome for a $90K MRR company with $180K in cash reserves. The question was how to satisfy compliance within 15 business days while minimizing the acquisition gap.

**Round 1** produced a wide spectrum of positions. Revenue opened with an aggressive 7-day surgical copy swap, arguing the 6-8 week rebuild estimate was a "product assumption masquerading as a legal requirement" and costing $29.5K more than necessary. Product Strategist reframed the crisis as a product-market fit problem — the flagged claims existed because BlendStack was marketing like a commodity supplement brand instead of selling its actual differentiator (the personalization engine). Technical Architect identified the root cause as a missing compliance gate in the content pipeline and insisted on a full-channel audit beyond the 9 cited claims. Contrarian named 7 unexamined assumptions, most critically that the FDA's net impression doctrine evaluates the entire creative unit (visual + copy + CTA), not just text — meaning a copy swap inside the same stressed-to-calm visual frame would likely fail. Compounder drew a red line against any fast relaunch of amended old creative, framing the compliance spend as an insurance premium on a policy BlendStack should have been paying all along. Moonshot pushed the boldest position: use the warning letter as a forcing function to initiate an IRB observational study protocol ($15-20K), transforming BlendStack's clinical credibility and exit multiple.

**Round 2** produced the synthesis. The CEO proposed a phased approach: genuinely new creative (not word-swapped old ads) built around the personalization engine, targeting a 10-14 day relaunch of 1-3 counsel-cleared ads. Revenue updated their position, conceding the net impression doctrine invalidated the 7-day text swap but defending the phased timeline as saving $23-26K in LTV versus full dark. Compounder dropped the red line against fast relaunch, accepting that new creative from a different emotional register is materially different from word-swapped old creative. The board converged on three critical conditions: counsel reviews the full creative unit (not just copy), no before/after emotional transformation visuals in Phase 1, and the full-channel audit findings must appear in the corrective action plan. Moonshot revised the IRB timing from Day 8 to Month 2, proposing a $5-7K design engagement as a compromise that starts the study clock 45 days earlier than a full 90-day delay.

The single largest unresolved risk is compliant creative conversion rate. The board acknowledged that personalization-led creative ("Know what's in your stack") may convert 30-50% worse than disease-claim creative ("Reduces anxiety in 7 days"). This variable cannot be priced before Day 14 and will determine whether Instagram remains a viable primary acquisition channel or whether BlendStack must restructure its channel mix around referral ($22 CAC) and organic ($8 CAC). Revenue set a clear trigger: if compliant creative CAC exceeds $95 at the 30-day mark, the channel strategy must be revisited. The board accepted this uncertainty as the cost of compliance — and as the beginning of a measurement that should have been run long ago.
