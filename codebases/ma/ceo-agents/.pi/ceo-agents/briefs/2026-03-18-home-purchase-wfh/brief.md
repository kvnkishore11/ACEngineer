# Brief: Should I buy a home now or wait — and which option maximizes my engineering career?

## Situation
I'm a senior software engineer (8 YOE) working fully remote, earning $195K base + $40K RSUs. My current lease in Austin, TX expires in 4 months. I've been renting a 1BR apartment at $2,100/month — no dedicated office, I work from the dining table. My productivity has dropped noticeably over the past year. I need a dedicated home office with proper desk setup, good lighting, sound isolation for calls, and room for a multi-monitor workstation. I'm evaluating three real home purchase options against continuing to rent. Interest rates are at 6.75% today, but there's speculation they'll drop to ~5.5% by Q1 2027. I have no other debt and strong credit (790).

## Stakes
If I buy now at 6.75%: I lock in a higher rate but start building equity immediately, get the office I need, and stop productivity bleed. If I wait 6-9 months for rates to drop: I save potentially $400-600/month on mortgage payments but continue working from a dining table, risk home prices increasing (Austin market is heating again), and my lease expires in 4 months so I'd need to sign another year. If I buy the wrong house: I'm locked into a 30-year commitment in a location that might not serve my career (what if I get an offer requiring relocation?). If I keep renting: I can get a 2BR for the office but I'm burning $2,400+/month with zero equity and no tax benefits. My engineering output and career trajectory are directly tied to my work environment — this isn't just a housing decision, it's an infrastructure decision for my career.

## Constraints
- Gross income: $195K base + $40K RSUs = $235K/year
- Savings: $120,000 (liquid, HYSA at 4.8%)
- Down payment budget: up to $80K (want to keep $40K emergency fund)
- Monthly housing budget: $3,200 max (mortgage + insurance + tax + HOA)
- Credit score: 790
- Current rate environment: 6.75% (30yr fixed), projected 5.5-6.0% by Q1 2027
- Lease expires: July 2026 (4 months)
- Work: 100% remote, no relocation expected but not contractually guaranteed
- Must-haves: dedicated office room, good internet infrastructure, quiet neighborhood
- Nice-to-haves: garage (for lab/workshop), yard, proximity to tech meetups
- Austin market: median home price up 4% YoY, inventory tightening in $350-450K range
- I plan to live in Austin for at least 3-5 years

## Key Question
Should I buy a home now at 6.75%, wait for rates to potentially drop, or optimize for a different path entirely — and which specific option best serves both my financial position and my engineering productivity?

## Proposed Direction
I'm leaning toward buying now in the $380-420K range, getting a 3BR with a dedicated office, and refinancing when rates drop. But I'm not sure if the math works or if I'm underestimating the risk. I want the board to stress-test this.
