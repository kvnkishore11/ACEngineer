# Brief: Which shorts platform should we lead with?

## Situation
We're a software-first supplement company selling customizable supplement stacks via a DTC app. Users build their own blends, we manufacture and ship. We have 2,000 active subscribers at $45/mo avg. Our content team is 2 people. We need to pick a primary short-form video platform to invest in for the next 6 months. Options: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, a multi-platform approach or something else entirely.

## Stakes
Short-form video is the #1 customer acquisition channel for DTC health brands right now. If we pick the right platform and nail the content, we could 3-5x subscriber growth in 6 months. If we spread too thin or pick wrong, we burn content budget with no growth and lose ground to competitors who are already posting daily.

## Constraints
- Content team: 2 people (1 creator, 1 editor)
- Content budget: $8K/month
- Our audience skews 25-40, health-conscious professionals
- We have zero existing following on any shorts platform
- Our product is visual (custom blends, packaging, results) but requires education to sell
- Regulatory: supplement claims must be careful (FDA)

## Key Question
Should we go all-in on one shorts platform, and if so which one — or should we repurpose across 2-3 platforms? What's the highest-ROI play for a 2-person content team with $8K/month? Should we do something else entirely?
