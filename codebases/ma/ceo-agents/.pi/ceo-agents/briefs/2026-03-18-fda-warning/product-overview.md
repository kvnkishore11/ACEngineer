# Product Overview — BlendStack

## What We Are
BlendStack is a DTC supplement company. Users build customizable supplement stacks through our app, and we manufacture and ship monthly.

## How It Works
1. User selects from 40+ ingredients (vitamins, minerals, adaptogens, aminos)
2. Our algorithm validates combinations for safety and efficacy
3. We manufacture to order in our Utah facility (FDA-registered, GMP-certified)
4. Ships in branded sachets — 30-day supply, auto-renews monthly

## Core Differentiator
Personalization at scale. Competitors sell fixed formulas. We let users design their own blend, backed by a dose-checking engine that prevents harmful interactions.

## Content That Got Flagged
The FDA warning specifically cited claims like:
- "Reduces anxiety naturally" (Instagram ad)
- "Boosts your immune system" (Instagram ad)
- "Improves sleep quality in 7 days" (Instagram ad)
- "Clinically proven ingredients for stress relief" (blog post)
- "Our adaptogens lower cortisol levels" (blog post)

These are disease/drug claims under FDA rules. Supplements can only make structure/function claims:
- OK: "Supports a calm mood" / "Helps maintain immune health"
- NOT OK: "Reduces anxiety" / "Boosts immunity"
